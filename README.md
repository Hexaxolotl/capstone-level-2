## The problem

	First time trainer is setting out on their adventure to become the best pokemon
	tamer. They want to find the best fit for their pokemon team. They will need to
	know which pokemon they are looking for, location, and the best time of day to encounter them.
	I am making this page to assit future pokemon trainers on their adventures.
	
	Do you want to be the very best?
	Is catching them all your real test?
	How about to train them, is that your cause?
	Is this your destiny?!
